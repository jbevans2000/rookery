The Rookery

Every raven, one roost

The Rookery is a small personal desktop app for Windows. Its owner uses it to read and manage their own Gmail and Google Workspace mailboxes in one window.

It shows each inbox, marks a letter as spam, unsubscribes from mailing lists, and sends replies, all through Google's Gmail API. It is not a public service: it is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.

A companion Chrome extension shows new-mail alerts. It talks only to the app running on the same computer.
