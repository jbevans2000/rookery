Privacy

The Rookery

The Rookery is a personal desktop app used only by its owner for the owner's own mailboxes. Mail is read and processed only on the owner's computer. It is never sent to any server run by the app, never shared with or sold to any third party, and never used for advertising or to train AI models. The app's only network contact is Google's own sign-in and Gmail services (and, when the owner presses Unsubscribe, the sender's own unsubscribe address). Sign-in tokens are stored locally on that computer, encrypted with Windows data protection. Removing an account in the app deletes its token, and access can also be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings.

Google user data

The Rookery's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
